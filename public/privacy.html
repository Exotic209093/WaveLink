WaveLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 2026  |  Last updated: February 2026

WaveLink ("the Extension") is a Chrome browser extension that helps Salesforce developers and administrators push, seed, and manage data directly from their browser. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

WaveLink collects and stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Salesforce org metadata – org IDs, instance URLs, usernames, and display names of orgs you connect.
  • Authentication tokens – OAuth 2.0 access tokens and session identifiers required to communicate with Salesforce APIs. These are stored in Chrome's local storage and are never transmitted to any server outside of Salesforce.
  • Schema cache – Salesforce object and field metadata fetched from your org, cached locally to improve performance.
  • Saved SOQL queries – Any SOQL queries you save within the Extension.
  • Push history – A record of data push operations you have performed, including file names, record counts, and outcomes.
  • User interface settings – Preferences such as theme and panel layout.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any data on our own servers.
  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or telemetry.
  • We do not access, read, or transmit the contents of files you upload beyond sending them directly to Salesforce on your behalf.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. How Data Is Used

All data collected by WaveLink is used solely to operate the Extension's features:

  • Authentication tokens are used exclusively to make authenticated API calls to Salesforce on your behalf.
  • Org metadata is displayed within the Extension UI so you can select and switch between orgs.
  • Schema cache data is used to populate field-mapping UIs and speed up data pushes.
  • Saved queries and push history are stored for your convenience so you can reuse and review past operations.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API, which means it is stored on your local device and is accessible only to this Extension. It is not synced to Chrome Sync or any external service.

Authentication tokens are scoped to Salesforce's OAuth flow and can be revoked at any time from within your Salesforce org's Connected Apps settings.

5. Third-Party Services

WaveLink communicates exclusively with Salesforce APIs (*.salesforce.com, *.force.com, *.lightning.force.com, *.my.salesforce.com, login.salesforce.com, test.salesforce.com). No data is sent to any other external service. The Salesforce platform's own Privacy Policy governs how Salesforce handles data on their platform.

6. Permissions Justification

  • storage – Required to save org connections, tokens, preferences, and push history locally.
  • cookies – Required to detect an active Salesforce session on pages you visit, so the Extension can authenticate without requiring you to log in again.
  • activeTab – Required to detect which Salesforce org you are currently viewing.
  • tabs – Required to open the WaveLink panel and communicate between the Extension and active Salesforce tabs.
  • host permissions (*.salesforce.com etc.) – Required to inject the content script that detects your Salesforce org and to make authenticated API requests.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all data stored by WaveLink at any time by:

  • Removing an individual org connection from within the Extension.
  • Using your browser's "Clear site data" or extension storage controls.
  • Uninstalling the Extension, which removes all locally stored data.

8. Children's Privacy

WaveLink is a developer tool intended for professional use. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and post the new policy at the same URL. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on GitHub or contact us via the Chrome Web Store listing.
